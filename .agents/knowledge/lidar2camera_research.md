# LiDAR-to-camera decisions

- The target-based checkerboard pipeline is the current release-oriented
  baseline.
- Targetless and learning-based calibration remain experimental; benchmark
  improvement or visual plausibility alone does not establish accuracy.
- Review extraction yield, geometry resolution, reprojection, holdout /
  repeatability, and visual overlays together.

Use the [target-based quick start](../../docs/lidar2camera_quickstart.md), the
[customer review guide](../../docs/lidar2camera_customer_guide.md), and the
[nuScenes benchmark guide](../../docs/lidar2camera_nuscenes_benchmark.md).
Detailed comparisons and experiments are retained in
[research notes](../notes/lidar2camera_research.md).
